A much bigger world: a chest of toys, water, a dog, mountains and birds. Everything he does still comes out of his own networks, and he learns every new skill from scratch, so you can watch him get better.

- **A chest of eight toys**: ball, blocks, bubbles, paper plane, boomerang, fishing rod, bicycle and marker, each with its own network and learning law; the guitar lives in the chest too. The «Каталог» (catalogue) screen shows them all, and «Предложить» (offer) suggests one: if he agrees, he plays with it right away, but whether he agrees is up to him.
- **A bigger, livelier world**: worlds of 640 and 1280 m, hunger, thirst and tiredness with food he picks by his own taste, ponds where he learns to swim from scratch and never drowns, fish, a diving mask, mountains he climbs his own way with a flag on the top, and flocks of birds. Speed goes up to x16.
- **A dog** that learns to walk by itself, can be tamed with patience and treats, and learns to bring the ball back from your praise.
- **He decides more by himself**: a navigator network chooses where to go, one boredom covers every activity (a guitar session now lasts 2-3 minutes instead of 27, and the guitar tires him faster than the toys), and «Положить» (put down) is a request he may refuse. The button appears for whatever he holds and only while he holds it.
- **Fixed**: walking left, the whole figure is mirrored, feet included; a deleted current world no longer comes back from the gallery; the bicycle no longer sinks into a steep hill; the old world stops completely, its sound fading in 25 ms, when you change the world or pause.
- Synthesised event sounds (hits, splashes, barking, a fanfare, chirping). 1.1.0 saves open, the guitar goes into the chest. The interface is in Russian.

**Downloads**

- `AiBoy-2.0.0-setup.exe` (37 MB) is the installer. It needs no admin rights and installs into the user profile; your saves stay in place.
- `AiBoy-2.0.0-portable.zip` (52 MB): unzip it and run `AiBoy.exe`. Everything is saved in the `saves` folder next to it.
